# Neo-Brutalism theme

This is the default and required theme.

## Visual recipe

- Canvas: warm paper (`--color-paper`), not pure white.
- Ink: almost-black (`--color-ink`) for borders, text, and hard shadows.
- Surfaces: flat fills using one of the accent colors; avoid gradients.
- Borders: 2–4 px, visible, square or very lightly rounded.
- Shadows: unblurred offsets (`4px 4px 0`, `8px 8px 0`, or `12px 12px 0`).
- Layout: visible grid, strong alignment, asymmetry with purpose, generous breathing room.
- Interaction: buttons physically move toward their shadow on press.

## Never do this by default

- Soft blurred shadows or floating white cards.
- Pale low-contrast text on pastel surfaces.
- Gradient backgrounds as decoration.
- Excessive rounded pills.
- Decorative style that makes the product harder to scan.
