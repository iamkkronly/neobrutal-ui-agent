# Card recipe

Cards are grouping devices, not default decoration.

- Use a clear heading and concise supporting text.
- Give the card a flat background and 3 px ink border.
- Use a hard shadow only when the card should feel elevated from its parent.
- Keep interactive cards keyboard reachable and give them a visible focus state.
- Avoid nested cards inside cards; use dividers or spacing instead.

Optional accent treatment: a small pixel mark or one colored edge. Keep the structural geometry Neo-Brutalist.
